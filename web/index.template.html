Method

From device data to a dose recommendation.

Collect

CGM every 5 min, smart-pen doses, meal log, steps, heart rate and sleep from a wearable, and the synthetic health record (diagnoses, labs including HbA1c and eGFR, genetics, prescriptions).

Personalise

An extended Bergman model starts from the record (weight, prescriptions, HbA1c, kidney function) and is fitted to the patient's own 5-hour forecasts (MAP, robust loss) in about a second. It learns how much a short night weakens insulin.

Synchronise

An observer keeps the twin locked to the CGM, so it always knows insulin on board, carbs on board, recent activity (steps or heart rate) and last night's sleep.

Decide

Every half-unit dose is simulated for 5 h and the lowest-risk safe dose is shown. At 23:00 the twin simulates the whole night and suggests a bedtime snack if a low is likely. Patients with a history of severe lows get a higher safety floor and earlier alerts.

    How to use DoseTwin

    DoseTwin builds a personal glucose–insulin model (a digital twin) for each person on insulin from their CGM, insulin-pen, meal, step, heart-rate and sleep data plus their health record. All patients here are synthetic.

    1. Patients: pick a patientThe list is sorted by hypoglycaemia risk, including tonight's bedtime check. Open a patient to see last week's glucose profile, their health record and wearables, and the twin's forecast for tonight.
    2. Try new settings on the virtual patientIn Settings review, click "Try suggestion" or move the carb ratio, correction and basal sliders. The twin replays a real day from last week so you can compare current and trial settings.
    3. Dose check: test a meal doseChoose a patient tab, drag the dose slider and compare the bolus calculator with the twin's recommendation. Click "Show what really happened" to see the true outcome of each dose.
    4. Evidence and MethodSee the held-out results against standard care and how the twin is built and validated.
