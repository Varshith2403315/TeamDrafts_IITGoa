DoseTwin
In-silico study · research prototype · not a medical device
Digital twin for insulin-treated diabetes · validated on type 1

Test the dose on your twin before you inject it.

DoseTwin learns a personal glucose–insulin model from 7 days of CGM, pen and step-counter data. At every meal it simulates the next five hours for each possible dose, picks the safest one that keeps glucose in range, and warns of lows before they start.

How it works

From devices to a dose

Collect

CGM every 5 min, smart-pen doses, meal photos or carb log, phone steps, and the clinic record (weight, carb ratio, correction factor, basal).

Personalise

An extended Bergman model starts from the clinic record and is fitted to the patient's own 5-hour forecasts (MAP, robust loss). It takes about a second per patient.

Synchronise

An observer keeps the twin locked to the CGM so it always knows insulin on board, carbs on board and recent activity.

Decide

Every half-unit dose is simulated for 5 h. Doses whose pessimistic path dips below 70 mg/dL are rejected; the lowest-risk safe dose is shown.
