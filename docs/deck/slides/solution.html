DoseTwin does three jobs

Adverse event

Checks the night at bedtime

At 23:00 the twin simulates the night. If a low is likely, it sizes a bedtime snack before the person falls asleep.

Adverse event

Warns before daytime lows

Twin forecast plus CGM trend, about 26 min ahead, while there is time to eat 15 g.

Personal treatment

Tests every meal dose

Simulates 5 hours for each half-unit dose and picks the lowest-risk dose above a safety floor.

Inputs: CGM · smart-pen doses · meal log · steps, heart rate and sleep · synthetic EHR (diagnoses, labs incl. HbA1c and eGFR, genetics, prescriptions).

This maps directly onto the brief: integrate wearable streams with the EHR, predict adverse events, and personalise the treatment protocol. DoseTwin is software, so it works with the affordable sensors families already use.
