DoseTwin does two jobs

Adverse event

Predicts lows before they start

Warns about hypoglycaemia while there is still time to eat 15 g of carbs, including during sleep.

Personal treatment

Tests every dose before injection

Simulates the next 5 hours for each half-unit dose and recommends the lowest-risk dose that stays above a safety floor.

Inputs: CGM every 5 min · smart-pen doses · meal log · phone steps · synthetic EHR (diagnoses, labs, genetics, prescriptions).

This maps directly onto the brief: integrate wearable streams with the EHR, predict adverse events, and personalise the treatment protocol. DoseTwin is software, so it works with the affordable sensors families already use.
