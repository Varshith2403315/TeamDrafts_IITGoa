The doctor's view

Patients ranked by hypoglycaemia risk, with the record, last week's glucose profile and the twin's suggested settings.

Move a setting and the virtual patient replays a real day under it.

Suggestions change at most 20% per review and are flagged when the twin is unsure.

This answers the brief's request for a conceptual doctor dashboard. Click a patient, try the twin suggestion, and show the predicted change in time in range and time below 70 on the replayed day.
