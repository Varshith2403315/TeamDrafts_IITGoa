AI and model details

ComponentWhat it is
Twin model10-state extended Bergman model: SC insulin, carb absorption, activity from steps + heart rate, sleep-debt insulin resistance, dawn effect
PersonalisationEHR prior (weight, prescriptions, HbA1c, eGFR), then MAP fit on 5–300 min forecast error
SynchronisationFixed-gain observer on CGM; unexplained-rate state captures surprises
Dose advisorKovatchev risk minimisation; rejects doses whose pessimistic path dips below 70 (80 with severe-hypo history)
Bedtime check8-h nominal and pessimistic night forecast at 23:00; flags below 84 mg/dL; snack sized on the twin
Low alertHybrid score: twin forecast minimum + 30-min CGM trend; alerts below 65
BaselineLightGBM forecaster, low classifier and night classifier, same inputs, trained on 30 other patients

The twin is interpretable: every parameter has a physiological meaning a clinician can review, and it can answer what if questions that a pure ML forecaster cannot.

Why mechanistic plus learning: a black-box forecaster predicts glucose but cannot simulate a dose it has never seen. The twin can, and its parameters map to clinical settings.
