AI and model details

ComponentWhat it is
Twin model10-state extended Bergman model: SC insulin, carb absorption, activity from steps, dawn effect
PersonalisationEHR prior (weight, insulin prescriptions, HbA1c), then MAP fit on 5–300 min forecast error
SynchronisationFixed-gain observer on CGM; unexplained-rate state captures surprises
Dose advisorKovatchev risk minimisation; rejects doses whose pessimistic path dips below 70
Low alertHybrid score: twin forecast minimum + 30-min CGM trend; alerts below 65
BaselineLightGBM forecaster and classifier trained on 30 other patients

The twin is interpretable: every parameter has a physiological meaning a clinician can review, and it can answer what if questions that a pure ML forecaster cannot.

Why mechanistic plus learning: a black-box forecaster predicts glucose but cannot simulate a dose it has never seen. The twin can, and its parameters map to clinical settings.
