Most accurate where it matters: near lows

Below 90 mg/dL, 60-min error is 19.9 mg/dL for the twin vs 23.3 for LightGBM. Overall, LightGBM is as good or better, and better at 2 h. The twin learns from 7 days of one person's data and can simulate doses; LightGBM cannot.

Be candid: the twin is not the best forecaster overall. It is competitive, best in the low range, and it is the only one that answers what-if questions.
