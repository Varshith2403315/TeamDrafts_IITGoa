How we tested it without fooling ourselves

Hidden truth

30 virtual patients from the published UVA/Padova T1D model. The twin uses a different model and never sees it.

Realistic life

Late Indian dinners, 30% carb-count error, missed boluses, mis-set clinic ratios, exercise, short nights, kidney disease, impaired awareness, sensor noise.

Held-out cohorts

All settings frozen on a development cohort. Results come from 3 fresh cohorts: 90 patient-weeks, 529 nights.

Paired arms

Every arm relives the same 7 days. Ablations refit the twin. Bootstrap CIs and Wilcoxon tests.

Key point for judges: tuning on the dev cohort gave a +5.5 point gain over premium CGM; on held-out cohorts it shrank. We report held-out numbers only.
