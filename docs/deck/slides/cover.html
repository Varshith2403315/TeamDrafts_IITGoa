Happiest Health Digital Twin Challenge 2026

DoseTwin

Test the dose on your twin before you inject it.

A personal digital twin for insulin-treated diabetes, validated on type 1. It checks tonight for lows at bedtime, warns before lows by day, and tests every meal dose, using CGM, pen, wearable and health-record data.

Team Drafts · Siva Sai Varshith Mitta, Abhijith Jayan · IIT Goa and KL University · Research prototype, evaluated in silico, not a medical device

Open with the one-line promise: test the dose on your twin before you inject it. Introduce the team and say up front that this is an in-silico proof of concept.
