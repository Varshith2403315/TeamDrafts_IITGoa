Method

From device data to a dose recommendation.

Collect

CGM every 5 min, smart-pen doses, meal log, phone steps, and the synthetic health record (diagnoses, labs including HbA1c, genetics, prescriptions).

Personalise

An extended Bergman model starts from the record (weight, prescriptions, HbA1c) and is fitted to the patient's own 5-hour forecasts (MAP, robust loss) in about a second.

Synchronise

An observer keeps the twin locked to the CGM, so it always knows insulin on board, carbs on board and recent activity.

Decide

Every half-unit dose is simulated for 5 h. Doses whose pessimistic path dips below 70 mg/dL are rejected; the lowest-risk safe dose is shown. Lows are predicted from the twin plus the CGM trend.

    How to use DoseTwin

    DoseTwin builds a personal glucose–insulin model (a digital twin) for each patient from their CGM, insulin-pen, meal and step data plus their health record. All patients here are synthetic.

    1. Patients: pick a patientThe list is sorted by hypoglycaemia risk. Open a patient to see last week's glucose profile, time-in-range metrics and their health record.
    2. Try new settings on the virtual patientIn Settings review, click "Try suggestion" or move the carb ratio, correction and basal sliders. The twin replays a real day from last week so you can compare current and trial settings.
    3. Dose check: test a meal doseChoose a patient tab, drag the dose slider and compare the bolus calculator with the twin's recommendation. Click "Show what really happened" to see the true outcome of each dose.
    4. Evidence and MethodSee the held-out results against standard care and how the twin is built and validated.
